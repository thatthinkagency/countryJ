Born in Ann Arbor. Raised in Buffalo. A Real American Story.

Country J isn't just another name in country music — he's the answer you've been waiting for. This 37-year-old, self-producing powerhouse brings an electrifying mix of authenticity, grit, and undeniable talent to the scene. Born in Ann Arbor and raised in Buffalo, Justin carries a rich blend of Midwest heart and Northeast hustle, giving his sound a refreshing edge that instantly sets him apart.

As the son of a preacher, storytelling runs through his veins. You can hear it in every lyric he writes and every note he sings — raw, soulful, and straight from the heart. His journey from small-town roots to the bright lights of New York City has shaped a style that resonates with fans everywhere.

With Country J, you're not just discovering a country artist — you're stepping into a living, breathing American story, told through music that moves you. Get ready to feel every word, every melody, and every moment.
